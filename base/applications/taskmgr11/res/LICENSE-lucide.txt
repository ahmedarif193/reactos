The in-application glyph outlines in icons.cpp (navigation, toolbar and row
icons) are the corresponding icons from Lucide (https://lucide.dev,
https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), embedded as pre-normalized path
data and used under the licenses reproduced below.  The window caption
glyphs (minimize/maximize/restore) are original drawings.

The application icon (taskmgr11.ico) is the classic ReactOS Task Manager
icon (GPL-2.0-or-later, from base/applications/taskmgr) and does not
derive from Lucide.
