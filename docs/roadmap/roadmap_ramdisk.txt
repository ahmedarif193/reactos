Notes
-----
- `/RDRAMSIZE=` specifies the requested minimum overlay; the loader automatically bumps it to `max(requested, iso_size + 64 MiB)` and refuses to build a writable image once that total plus safety slack exceeds the ~1 GiB low-memory budget on 32-bit FreeLDR.
- Current blocker: pool corruption in the CDROM path causes a `0x19` bugcheck while `IoCreateArcNamesDisk` links the boot volume. Use DV/Special Pool to capture the overwriting stack before proceeding with further validation.
