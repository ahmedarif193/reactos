SList hardening parity for ARM64 – notes
========================================

- Mirror amd64/i386 behavior:
  - Add KiHandleKernelSListFaultArm64 that traps SList faults, checks canonical Next, and bugchecks on corruption (no silent retry).
  - Harden interlocked SList primitives to work with 16-byte headers when adding cookies/ABA tags.

- Optional diagnostics (DBG/Verifier):
  - Cookie stamping on push and verification on pop to catch stomps while entries sit on the list.
  - Per-head shadow metadata (last push/pop caller/tag/size) for attribution.
  - Lookaside bypass flag for triage (e.g., ExpDisablePoolLookaside equivalent) and tag-based skips.

- Controls:
  - Boot/registry toggle to enable/disable hardening/logging without rebuild.

- Target tags to watch based on amd64 experience:
  - USB hub/port small allocations (tags UHUB/usbp) if corruption resurfaces on ARM64.
