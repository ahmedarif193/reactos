CC decision
-----------
The ARM64 CC workaround was saved here:
  OPENCODE_CC_COPY_ARM64_PREFAULT_WORKAROUND.patch

Do not push that workaround as the proper fix. It pre-faults VACB system-cache
pages in CcCopyRead/CcCopyWrite, which papers over the lower MM contract.

The correct contract is:
  CcCopyRead/CcCopyWrite gets a valid VACB and resident data, then normal
  kernel access to Vacb->BaseAddress should fault cleanly through MM.

The correct fix belongs in ARM64 MM/system-cache fault handling, not common CC.
At handoff time, ntoskrnl/cc/copy.c has no active diff from this workaround.
